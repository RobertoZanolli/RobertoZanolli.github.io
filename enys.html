// overview

enys is a web application that combines artificial intelligence with fitness tracking. it's designed to be your complete fitness companion — generating personalized workout splits, tracking your weight and progress over time, and providing ai-powered insights to help you train smarter.

the app features multiple ai-powered tools working together: a conversational chatbot interface for natural interactions, an intelligent workout split generator that creates personalized routines, weight tracking with visual progress charts, and an insights engine that analyzes your data to surface meaningful patterns and recommendations.
